The problem

Many families skip care because they cannot pay for it.

Low-income families struggle to afford medicines, medical checkups, laboratory tests, and treatments. Some choose not to visit a hospital or buy their prescribed medicines at all.

Prescriptions go unfilled

Medicine costs lead people to leave prescribed treatment unbought.

Checkups get postponed

Consultation and laboratory fees keep families away from hospitals.

Illness gets worse

Untreated conditions can turn into more serious health problems.

Our solution

Communities and care providers working together.

We provide affordable medicines and free medical assistance programs. Communities work with local government units, hospitals, pharmacies, and health organizations to bring care within reach.
